## Why two data-catalog platforms are in a retrieval bundle

**Naming, not technology.** Two established projects with a combined ~27k stars independently
rewrote their taglines around the word **"context"** during 2026. Neither is a retrieval server
and neither competes with Tarn functionally.

But the category label is being claimed, from three directions at once:

- **Metadata platforms** → "context platform" / "context layer" (these two).
- **Agent tooling** → [Context7](../documents/context7.md) owns "context for agents" at ~60k
  stars.
- **Agent memory** → [mem0, Zep, Letta](../general/agent-memory-tier.md) own "memory" at 100k+
  combined.

**The consequence for Tarn's positioning:** both obvious labels are taken. Calling Tarn a
"context server" puts it in a phrase already carrying two unrelated meanings; calling it a
"memory server" invites benchmarking against mem0 on recall of agent-authored facts, which is
the wrong axis entirely.

The differentiated and still-unclaimed framing is concrete and literal:

> **Ranked retrieval over your own files — notes, code, PDFs — offline, with no API keys.**

It says what the corpus is (yours, on disk), what the operation is (ranked retrieval, not
storage or memory), and what the constraint is (offline, no keys) — all of which are
checkable, and none of which are contested.
